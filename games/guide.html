How to use W.H. Academy

A quick guide to everything on your dashboard. Tap any section to open it.

🧭 Finding your way

On a phone, the bar at the bottom has three tabs — Classes, Home (in the middle) and Progress. Everything else — Animations, Boss Battle, Revision, Ranks, Advanced Games & Advanced Ranks (Max plan), Badges, Support, Guide, Profile, Change Plan, Theme and Log out — lives inside the menu (☰) at the top-right, next to your notifications 🔔.

On a computer, the same places are listed down the sidebar on the left.

🏠 Dashboard — your home

This is your starting screen. It shows your name and class, your subjects, and a few quick numbers (XP, day streak, revision due).

  1. My Subjects — the subjects you enrolled in. Tap one to see its chapters.
  2. Ready for something new — a shortcut to pick your next chapter.
  3. Focus Areas / Your chapters — fills in once you start playing, showing where you are strong and what to practise.
🧾 Fee — your monthly invoices

The Fee tab shows your fee month by month. Each month card shows the total; tap it to see the full invoice — your dashboard fee (from your plan and subjects) and, if you take online / live classes, that fee added on, with the total.

  • Please submit your fee between the 1st and the 3rd of each month.
  • Your first month is charged only for the days left in that month, counting from the day your account was activated (the activation day counts). From the next month the full monthly fee applies.
  • If the current month stays unpaid, your dashboard is locked from the 5th. You will see a screen with a WhatsApp button — if you have already paid, message W.H. Academy with your Name, Father name and Class (Student ID too if you know it) and your payment screenshot. Once it is confirmed, your dashboard opens again automatically.
  • When your fee is marked paid, you get a notification. On the 1st of each month a new voucher appears here with an Unpaid tag until it is paid.
Already-paid months never change, even if the fee is revised later. A fee change always applies from the next month, and you get a notification about it.
📘 Chapters & games — how to study
  1. Open Chapters from the menu (or tap a subject on the dashboard).
  2. Pick a chapter. Chapters marked Coming soon are not ready yet.
  3. Inside a chapter, play the games for each topic. Every correct answer earns XP.
  4. Your performance per chapter builds up on the dashboard so you can see what to revise.
Only the subjects you enrolled in unlock. If a subject is missing, contact W.H. Academy to add it.
🎬 Animations — see the solutions move

The Animations tab turns your book's exercises into step-by-step animated solutions. The question and a moving diagram are on the left; the full working builds up on the right.

  1. Open Animations, pick a chapter, then a tab (Theory, or a section like 7.1, 7.2 or 7.3), then a question — each section has Worked Examples, Practise Now and the book's Exercise questions.
  2. Press ▶ Play to watch it build. Auto plays it like a video; Manual waits for you at every step (use ▶ or the → key). The dots jump to any step.
  3. Speed it up or slow it down with 1× / 2× / 3×.
  4. Tap the cube on the drawing for a real 3D model of problems like the ladder, the pole and cable, the tent, the ships and the courier's journey. Drag to walk around it, then play with it: Climb the kid up the ladder, Fly the bird along the cable, Sail the boats, slide the slider to change the numbers, and jump to Front · Side · Top, Spin it or switch to Night. Every measurement (like AC = 2.4 m) has a small label that tells you what it is.
  5. Zoom with the + / − buttons, a pinch, or Ctrl + scroll. A normal scroll always scrolls the page, so you never get stuck.
  6. Change the Theme — from the menu (☰) on a phone, or the sidebar on a computer — to change the look; a calmer or dark theme helps if the screen feels too bright.
  7. Open Settings to make lines or text thicker, shade the shapes, switch the grid off (it is on by default so you can read lengths) or turn Labels along the lines off if you prefer them flat.
  8. Use Prev / Next on the question, or the List button, to move between questions without going back. In the list, drag its left edge (or tap the expand button) to widen it so each question shows in full.
  9. On a computer, drag the bar in the middle to make the drawing or the solution bigger.
  10. In Chemistry the drawings are displayed formulae: the carbons, hydrogens and bonds appear one by one. Tap a colour in the legend to spotlight a part (the C=C, the –OH, the –COOH), use the Values tab for sliders and choices (number of carbons, single / double / triple bond), and watch the atoms really move when an ester forms.
  11. Finish the challenges under Try it to check you understood.

Animations are part of the Max plan, and only your enrolled subjects appear. The library grows chapter by chapter — Class 8 Maths Chapter 7 (Pythagoras' Theorem) is complete, with every question of Exercise 7A, 7B and 7C. Class 10 Chemistry (Federal Board) Unit 8, Organic Chemistry is now in too: 16 theory lessons, the Worked Examples and every question of Concept Assessment Exercises 8.1 to 8.6. It is shown to Federal Board students only.

🛡️ Boss Battle — test yourself

A Boss Battle is a bigger challenge (a paper set by your teacher) that checks how well you have learned a chapter, subject, or whole class.

  1. Open Boss Battle from the menu.
  2. Pick an available battle and attempt it like a test.
  3. Submit when done. Your teacher reviews it and releases your result.
KPK board papers (Class 10): if you are enrolled in the KPK board, some Boss Battle papers are full board-pattern exams. First you fill your 6-digit Roll Number in circles, one circle in every column from 0 to 9, exactly like the board OMR sheet (it is compulsory and must match your roll number). Then Section A (12 MCQs on an OMR-style sheet — fill the circle) with 20 minutes; Sections B (any 8 of 11 short questions, up to 2000 characters each) and C (any 3 of 4 long questions, each with 2 parts, up to 6000 characters per part) get 2 hours 40 minutes, so the whole paper is 3 hours. A timer stays at the top-right corner of the screen while you scroll, and the paper is submitted automatically when it reaches zero. In B and C use the toolbar to write a heading and to make key words bold, underlined, italic or coloured, so your paper is neat and easy to read. You can press Go to Sections B & C early, go back to the MCQs any time, and submit before the time ends. Your answers are saved on your device automatically, so a power cut, lost internet or closed page loses nothing — open the paper again on the same phone/computer and browser and carry on. Nothing is sent to your teacher until you press Submit (or the time ends). MCQs are checked straight away; your teacher marks B and C.
If you are in Class 9–12, every Boss Battle paper shows your Roll Number at the top (taken from your Student ID), just like a board exam paper.
Keep your overall practice up — your average progress feeds into your Boss Battle readiness.
🏅 Badges — rewards for effort

Badges are little rewards you unlock automatically as you hit milestones — finishing chapters, keeping streaks, scoring well, and more.

  1. Open Badges to see what you have earned and what is still locked.
  2. Each locked badge tells you what to do to unlock it. Just keep learning.
🔁 Revision — remember what you learned

Revision brings back questions you have seen before, at the right time, so they stick in your memory.

  1. Open Revision. The number of items due shows on your dashboard.
  2. Do your due items daily — a few minutes keeps your memory strong.
🏆 Ranks — your XP & standing

XP (experience points) is what you earn for correct answers and activity. Ranks shows your XP in each game type and your progress toward the Boss Battle.

  1. Open Ranks to see your totals.
  2. Play more games to raise your XP and climb.
⚡ Advanced Games & Advanced Ranks (Max plan)

Students on the Max plan (Class 6 to 12) get two extra tabs: Advanced Games and Advanced Ranks. There are two games: Neon Racer and Auto Defense. In both you answer your own class questions, climb a rank ladder, earn coins and unlock new looks.

  1. Open Advanced Games and tap Play on a game. Your first run is a short Placement Run (15 questions) that puts you on the right level.
  2. The first time you open each game on a device, a How to play card shows you the controls. You can open it again any time from Settings or the three-line menu.
  3. Switch games without leaving: inside either game use Switch to Auto Defense (or Switch to Neon Racer) on the home, pause or result screen, or the three-line menu (☰) → Advanced games. If a run is paused, the game asks before you leave it.
  4. The same menu takes you to every dashboard tab, plus Settings, Garage / Armory and How to play. While a run is paused you can also open Settings straight from the Pause screen.
  5. Open Advanced Ranks to see the leaderboard for your class ladder (see the last section below).

Where the questions come from: the games use the same question banks as your chapter games (Easy, Medium and Hard). Class 6 to 8 get questions from every subject of their class. Class 9 to 12 get questions only from the subjects they are enrolled in. Formulas and equations are shown properly in the questions and answers. If your class does not have its own bank yet, the game uses the nearest class's questions until it does.

Advanced Ranks is separate from the normal Ranks page: Ranks shows your XP from the regular games, Advanced Ranks shows your Game Rank from the Advanced Games. They never mix. Both games share the same level, rank, coins and unlocks.

The tabs below explain each part in detail: Neon Racer, Auto Defense, hearts and power-ups, levels and Rank Gates, all ranks, rank drop, unlocks, settings and the leaderboard.
🏎️ Neon Racer — how to play

You drive a car down a four-lane road. The question is at the top, and the four answers (A to D) are gates across the lanes. Drive through the gate with the right answer.

  • Phone: use the on-screen Left and Right buttons to change lane and the orange GO button to drive up to the gates. You can also tap an answer, or tap the left or right side of the road.
  • Computer: arrow keys. Left/Right steer, Up drives forward, Down brakes. Keys 1 to 4 pick an answer, Space = go.
  • Right gate: a clean pass and a speed boost. Wrong gate or out of time: a crash, the car jolts, straightens up and carries on, and you lose a heart (in Ranked and Placement runs).
  • Landscape: turn your phone sideways to play wide. The question and power-ups move to the left, the answers to the right, the road stays in the middle.
  • Day or night: tap the sun/moon button. Night adds stars, street lamps and a moon. If you unlock Headlights, you can switch beams on for night driving in the Garage.
  • Scenery: Highway, City, Off-road, Snow ridge and Coast, each with its own day and night look. Pick one in the Garage (more unlock as your rank grows).
  • Sound: engine hum and ambient sounds (birds in the day, breeze and crickets at night) can each be switched off in Settings.
🛡️ Auto Defense — how to play

Four drones (A to D) float down towards your base, each carrying one answer. Your base has a tower, a shield dome and three turrets.

  • Aim: tap the drone, or its answer button at the bottom, to lock your turrets on it. Computer: keys 1 to 4, or Left/Right arrows to move the aim.
  • Fire: with Confirm tap on (Settings), the first tap only aims and a second tap on the same drone fires (Space on a computer). With Confirm tap off, one tap fires. Class 6 to 8 start with Confirm tap on (fewer wrong taps); you can switch it off in Settings.
  • Right drone: destroyed, and the rest of the squad collapses. Wrong drone or out of time: your base is hit and you lose a heart (in Ranked and Placement runs).
  • Rank Gate: during a gate the drones wear red-and-gold boss armour, the edges glow red, and a RANK GATE bar shows your right answers so far (one block each) and your strikes (three crosses; the third mistake fails the gate).
  • Landscape: in sideways mode the question and power-ups sit on the left, the answers on the right, and the battlefield stays in the middle.
  • Day or night scenery and ambient sounds work the same as in Neon Racer. Your Armory holds turret paint, beam colour and shot effect (they unlock with rank).
  • Computer shortcuts: F = 50:50, T = freeze, H = heal, S = skip, P = pause, N = day/night.
❤️ Hearts, streaks, Think-Slow & power-ups
  • Modes: Ranked Run counts for your level and rank. Practice never ends the run (no hearts lost) and never changes your level or rank, so use it to learn. Placement Run is the first 15-question run that places you.
  • Hearts: you start every Ranked run with 3 hearts. A wrong answer or running out of time costs 1 heart. At 0 hearts the run ends (Game Over).
  • Streak: every right answer in a row builds your streak. A wrong answer resets it to 0. A streak also multiplies your XP: 3 in a row ×1.2, 6 ×1.5, 10 ×1.8, 15 ×2.0.
  • Heart regain: reach a 10-answer streak and you win 1 heart back (if you are below 3, and not during a Rank Gate).
  • Think-Slow: while you read and think, the game world slows down so you are not rushed. The speed drops to 35% in the Teal ranks, 45% in Violet, 55% in Magenta and 65% in Gold, so higher ranks get a faster, tougher game.
  • Thinking time per question: Class 9 to 12: Easy 15 s, Medium 30 s, Hard 60 s. Class 6 to 8: Easy 10 s, Medium 15 s, Hard 25 s. Calculation questions get +50%. Higher rank bands shorten the time by 7%, 14% and 20%. Switch on Extra reading time in Settings for ×1.5.
  • Power-ups (one of each per run, shown above the answers):
    • 50:50 removes two wrong answers.
    • Freeze stops time and adds extra seconds (20 s for Class 9 to 12, 10 s for Class 6 to 8).
    • Heal gives you 1 heart (only if you are below 3).
    • Skip skips the question: no points, no heart lost, streak kept.
📈 Levels, XP & Rank Gates
  • Levels: Class 9 to 12 climb 100 levels (20 ranks). Class 6 to 8 climb 60 levels (12 ranks). Each rank covers several levels.
  • XP bar: every right answer fills the bar of your current level: Easy 10, Medium 14, Hard 20 points, times your streak multiplier. When the bar is full you go up one level. Later levels need a bigger bar.
  • Rank Gate: at the last level of a rank the bar stops at full and a Rank Gate opens. Beat the gate to reach the next rank. Home shows Retry Rank Gate if a gate is waiting.
  • How to pass: answer enough questions right without making a 3rd mistake. You can be wrong at most 2 times. You need 10 right in the Teal ranks, 12 in Violet, 15 in Magenta and 18 in Gold. A gate always starts with full hearts.
  • Fail a gate? The bar stays full and you can retry as often as you like. Nothing is lost.
  • Rank up: a celebration screen shows your new badge, the new scenery theme and anything you just unlocked.
🏅 All ranks, names, colours & badges

Ranks are grouped in four colour bands of five. The band decides your colour, how hard the game is and how many answers you need at the Rank Gate.

  • Teal ranks 1 to 5 · Violet ranks 6 to 10 · Magenta ranks 11 to 15 · Gold ranks 16 to 20.
  • Badges: Neon Racer shows a shield with a speed stripe; Auto Defense shows a hexagon core. Inside, the number of chevrons (Racer) or rings (Defense) shows your place in the band (1 to 5). Gold ranks also wear a crown.

📉 Rank drop, grace & comeback
  • Class 6 to 8: there is no penalty. A wrong answer never lowers your bar, level or rank.
  • Class 9 to 12: a wrong answer takes a small part of your bar (about a third of that question's points) outside Rank Gates. If the bar goes below empty you may drop one level, but never below the first level of your current rank.
  • Grace: after every rank-up you get 30 answers with no bar penalty, so a new rank starts safely.
  • Rank drop (Class 9 to 12 only): it happens only if you are on the first level of a rank, out of grace, and finish two Ranked runs in a row (10+ answers each) under 40% right after a warning. You then move to the top level of the rank below and get a comeback boost.
  • Comeback boost: after a rough patch (fewer than 10 right in your last 20) a streak of 3 gives your next 10 right answers +0.5 on the multiplier.
The game warns you before any drop. Use Practice to get back on track at no risk.

⚙️ Settings & options

Open Settings from the game home, the Pause screen or the three-line menu.

  • Scenery: day or night.
  • Graphics: Auto, Low, Medium or High. Auto lowers the detail by itself if your phone struggles.
  • Sound and Vibration on or off.
  • Confirm tap (second tap fires), Left-handed layout, Extra reading time (×1.5), Colour-blind mode, Reduce motion.
  • Text size: Small, Normal or Large.
  • Engine hum (Racer) and Ambient sounds (both games). These are saved on this device only.
  • On-screen buttons (Racer): Auto shows Left / Right / GO on touch screens only, Always show and Hide force it. Also saved per device, so you can use buttons on your phone and the keyboard on a laptop.
  • How to play: opens the controls card again.
🪙 Game coins & the Advanced Ranks leaderboard
  • Game coins: 3 coins for every right answer and +25 for every rank-up. They are separate from your academy coins.
  • Advanced Ranks lists the top players on your class ladder (Class 9 to 12 together, Class 6 to 8 together) for the game you choose. You are marked YOU, and only nicknames are shown, never real names.
  • Options on the page: show the Top 10, Top 20 or Top 50; filter by rank group (Spark – Flux, Volt – Vortex, Aura – Zenith, Titan – Omega); search a nickname; or tap Jump to me to find your row. Long nicknames show in full on a phone.
  • Your position comes from your level. Keep playing Ranked runs to climb.
🔥 XP, streaks & coins
  • XP — points for learning. More practice = more XP.
  • Day streak — how many days in a row you studied. Log in and do a little each day to keep it alive. Turn on notifications (see the next section) to get a daily reminder so you never break it.
  • Coins — earned through activity and spent on in-app rewards where available.
🔔 Notifications — turn on reminders & alerts

W.H. Academy can remind you to study, keep your day streak alive, and alert you when a Boss Battle result is ready — even when the app is closed. You turn this on once on each device you use.

Step 1 — turn it on inside the app (works on every device):

  1. Open your Dashboard.
  2. Tap the bell icon 🔔 at the top-right of the screen.
  3. Tap “Enable device alerts”.
  4. When your phone or computer asks for permission, tap Allow.

That is all most people need. If alerts still don't arrive, your browser or device is blocking them — follow the steps for your device below.

📱 iPhone or iPad (needs iOS 16.4 or newer):

  1. Open the website in Safari, tap the Share button, then “Add to Home Screen”.
  2. Open W.H. Academy from that new home-screen icon (not from Safari), then do Step 1 above. On iPhone, web alerts only work when opened from the home-screen icon.
  3. Still off? Go to Settings → Notifications → W.H. Academy → Allow Notifications: ON.
  4. Turn off Focus / Do Not Disturb when you want to be reminded.

🤖 Android phone (any brand — Samsung, Xiaomi, Oppo, Infinix, etc.):

  1. Open the website in Chrome. Best results: Chrome menu ⋮ → “Add to Home screen / Install app”, then open it from the new icon.
  2. Do Step 1 above and tap Allow.
  3. Still off? Phone Settings → Apps → Chrome (or W.H. Academy) → Notifications → ON.
  4. Some phones save battery hard — set that app's battery to “No restrictions / Don't optimise” so reminders arrive on time.

🪟 Windows laptop or PC (Chrome or Edge):

  1. Do Step 1 above and click Allow.
  2. If the browser already blocked it: click the 🔒 lock icon in the address bar → Notifications → Allow, then refresh the page.
  3. Open Windows Settings → System → Notifications and make sure notifications are On and your browser is allowed.
  4. Turn off Focus assist / Do not disturb to see the alerts.

🍎 Mac (Chrome, Edge or Safari):

  1. Do Step 1 above and click Allow.
  2. If the browser already blocked it: click the 🔒 lock icon in the address bar → Notifications → Allow, then refresh the page.
  3. Open System Settings → Notifications → (your browser) and turn Allow Notifications: ON.
  4. Turn off Do Not Disturb / Focus in Control Centre to see the alerts.
You only do this once per device. To stop alerts later, tap the bell again and choose “Turn off device alerts”.
↩️ Support & Rechecking — if a result looks wrong

If you think a Boss Battle result was marked incorrectly, you can ask for a recheck.

  1. Open Support & Rechecking.
  2. Choose the result you want rechecked and submit your request with a short reason.
  3. Your teacher reviews it and updates your result if needed. You will see the outcome here.
📈 My Progress

See your learning over time — weekly, monthly and lifetime — and which chapters or topics need more work. Use it to plan what to study next.

👤 Profile, PIN & subjects
  • Profile shows your details and subjects.
  • To change your PIN, use the Forgot/Change PIN option on the login screen — a code is emailed to the address on file.
  • To add or change subjects, contact W.H. Academy.
  • Requests (in Settings, at the bottom): you or your parent can ask to leave W.H. Academy, to correct wrong information (name, class, WhatsApp number and so on) or to delete your data. Tap the one you need, fill in the short form and send it. W.H. Academy handles it and tells you the result in Notifications and in the My requests list on the same page. You can cancel a request that is still waiting.
  • Leaving closes your account after approval, and fees already paid are not returned. Your records may be kept for record-keeping and backup. If you want your data deleted, send a Delete my data request: we then delete your personal details and learning activity and keep only minimal fee and payment records.
🎓 Moving to the next class each year

At the start of a new academic year, you don't re-enrol. When W.H. Academy promotes you, your dashboard will ask you to pick your new class's stream, subjects and plan.

  1. Log in — you'll see a short "You have been promoted" form.
  2. Choose your stream and subjects, pick a plan, and submit.
  3. Once W.H. Academy approves it, your new class opens. Your PIN stays the same.

If something isn't working or you can't find what you need, contact W.H. Academy and we'll help you out.
