How to use W.H. Academy

A quick guide to everything on your dashboard. Tap any section to open it.

🏠 Dashboard — your home

This is your starting screen. It shows your name and class, your subjects, and a few quick numbers (XP, day streak, revision due).

  1. My Subjects — the subjects you enrolled in. Tap one to see its chapters.
  2. Ready for something new — a shortcut to pick your next chapter.
  3. Focus Areas / Your chapters — fills in once you start playing, showing where you are strong and what to practise.
📘 Chapters & games — how to study
  1. Open Chapters from the menu (or tap a subject on the dashboard).
  2. Pick a chapter. Chapters marked Coming soon are not ready yet.
  3. Inside a chapter, play the games for each topic. Every correct answer earns XP.
  4. Your performance per chapter builds up on the dashboard so you can see what to revise.
Only the subjects you enrolled in unlock. If a subject is missing, contact W.H. Academy to add it.
🛡️ Boss Battle — test yourself

A Boss Battle is a bigger challenge (a paper set by your teacher) that checks how well you have learned a chapter, subject, or whole class.

  1. Open Boss Battle from the menu.
  2. Pick an available battle and attempt it like a test.
  3. Submit when done. Your teacher reviews it and releases your result.
Keep your overall practice up — your average progress feeds into your Boss Battle readiness.
🏅 Badges — rewards for effort

Badges are little rewards you unlock automatically as you hit milestones — finishing chapters, keeping streaks, scoring well, and more.

  1. Open Badges to see what you have earned and what is still locked.
  2. Each locked badge tells you what to do to unlock it. Just keep learning.
🔁 Revision — remember what you learned

Revision brings back questions you have seen before, at the right time, so they stick in your memory.

  1. Open Revision. The number of items due shows on your dashboard.
  2. Do your due items daily — a few minutes keeps your memory strong.
🏆 Ranks — your XP & standing

XP (experience points) is what you earn for correct answers and activity. Ranks shows your XP in each game type and your progress toward the Boss Battle.

  1. Open Ranks to see your totals.
  2. Play more games to raise your XP and climb.
🔥 XP, streaks & coins
  • XP — points for learning. More practice = more XP.
  • Day streak — how many days in a row you studied. Log in and do a little each day to keep it alive.
  • Coins — earned through activity and spent on in-app rewards where available.
↩️ Support & Rechecking — if a result looks wrong

If you think a Boss Battle result was marked incorrectly, you can ask for a recheck.

  1. Open Support & Rechecking.
  2. Choose the result you want rechecked and submit your request with a short reason.
  3. Your teacher reviews it and updates your result if needed. You will see the outcome here.
📈 My Progress

See your learning over time — weekly, monthly and lifetime — and which chapters or topics need more work. Use it to plan what to study next.

👤 Profile, PIN & subjects
  • Profile shows your details and subjects.
  • To change your PIN, use the Forgot/Change PIN option on the login screen — a code is emailed to the address on file.
  • To add or change subjects, contact W.H. Academy.
🎓 Moving to the next class each year

At the start of a new academic year, you don't re-enrol. When W.H. Academy promotes you, your dashboard will ask you to pick your new class's stream, subjects and plan.

  1. Log in — you'll see a short "You have been promoted" form.
  2. Choose your stream and subjects, pick a plan, and submit.
  3. Once W.H. Academy approves it, your new class opens. Your PIN stays the same.
❓ Still stuck?

If something isn't working or you can't find what you need, contact W.H. Academy and we'll help you out.
