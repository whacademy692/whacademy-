Who We Are

Our Story

W.H. Academy is an online and home tutoring platform based in Rawalpindi, Pakistan, founded by M. Rehan Safdar — a Mathematics specialist with a BS Mathematics degree and teaching experience at Army Public School.

The academy was built around a simple idea: give students focused, exam-ready teaching in the subjects that matter most, without the noise. We provide Mathematics and Science tutoring for Class 6 to 8, Matriculation, and FSc students following the Federal Board (FBISE) curriculum — the same standards our free notes are aligned with, so classroom and self-study reinforce each other. Our notes and learning games are also a strong fit for APSACS students.
