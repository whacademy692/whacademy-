Before you write — a few quick tips

  • Write in your own words. You can just type it yourself — it does not need to be perfect.
  • Any language is welcome. Roman Urdu (like your WhatsApp messages), simple Urdu, or English — all three are fine. Writing from another country? Use any language you like.
  • Using AI to structure your response? Great — but AI often adds extra blank lines that make the card very long, with big empty gaps. Please delete those extra blank lines before you submit so it looks clean.
