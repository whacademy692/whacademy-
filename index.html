About W.H. Academy

By the grace of Allah, W.H. Academy is an online and home tutoring platform based in Rawalpindi, Pakistan, founded by M. Rehan Safdar, a Mathematics specialist with a BS Mathematics degree and teaching experience at Army Public School. The academy provides Mathematics and Science tutoring for Class 6 to 8 following the APSACS middle-school curriculum (Cambridge-based / Cambridge-aligned), and for Matriculation and FSc students following the Federal Board (FBISE) curriculum. Students from other Pakistani boards or international curricula can also be supported through a personalized pathway after their syllabus is reviewed. Services include recorded video lectures, chapter-wise quizzes, WhatsApp support, and monthly assessments, with plans ranging from ₨2,000 to ₨12,000 per month depending on grade level and subject count. May Allah keep us sincere in this mission and grant success to every student.

How to create your account

Never registered before? Don't worry, just follow these steps in order. You'll enroll first, and then set up your login. It only takes a few minutes.

1
Open the enrollment form
Tap the Get Student ID button below. This opens the enrollment form.
2
Fill it in & choose your plan
Complete the form and select your class and subjects. Your selection decides your fee.
3
Confirm your fee on WhatsApp
Send us a WhatsApp message to confirm your selected fee, and let us know once you have submitted the payment.
How to pay your fee
Send your fee via EasyPaisa or JazzCash to 0340‑0876011 — both accounts are on this same number. After paying, share the payment screenshot with us on WhatsApp to confirm.

4
Your account is activated
Once we confirm your payment, we set your status to Active.
5
Receive your Student ID
We email your Student ID to the address you provided. The same email also contains a link to the registration form.
6
Complete the registration form
Open the link from that email and fill in the registration form.
7
Set your PIN
You'll receive another email. Use it to set your account PIN.
8
Verify with OTP
The same email sends you an OTP. Enter it to verify your account.
9
Log in, you're done!
Once your OTP is verified, log in with your Student ID and PIN. Your account is ready, tap Start Playing and jump in.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does W.H. Academy cost?

Middle School plans range from ₨5,000 to ₨12,000 per month covering all subjects. Matric and FSc plans are charged per subject, from ₨2,000 to ₨5,500 per subject per month.

Who teaches at W.H. Academy?

W.H. Academy is led by M. Rehan Safdar (BS Mathematics, Army Public School teaching experience) and Shumaila Bibi (Science, History & Geography instructor).

What curriculum does W.H. Academy follow?

Middle School and Matric/FSc programs follow the Federal Board (FBISE) curriculum.

Is W.H. Academy online or in-person?

Both. Online tuition is available for all students across Pakistan. Home tuition is available in Rawalpindi.
