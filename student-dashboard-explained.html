How a student gets in

Enrol using the form and choose your plan. No PIN is needed yet.
We activate your account after the fee is confirmed and email your Student ID.
Set your PIN with the link in the email (a code confirms it is you).
Log in with your Student ID and PIN — the dashboard opens.

Details: How It Works.

The dashboard, tab by tab

Each tab below shows what is inside, how it works and how it helps. The pills show which plans include it — most tabs are in every plan. Animations is part of Max, and rechecking and the Past Papers shelf are part of Plus and Max.

Dashboard

Menu: Dashboard (your home screen)
Basic Plus Max

What you’ll find

  • Your name, class, plan and subjects on one card
  • An Upgrade Plan button at the top of the menu (Max students see Change Plan)
  • Quick numbers: Total XP, Day streak and items due for revision
  • Continue shortcut to pick up where you left off
  • Focus Areas — where you are strong and what to practise
  • Recent achievements and certificates

How it works

  1. Log in with your Student ID and PIN — you land here.
  2. Tap a subject in My Subjects to see its chapters.
  3. The numbers and focus areas update by themselves as you study.
  4. Tap the bell at the top-right for reminders (see Notifications).

How it helps

Students see in ten seconds what to do today. Nothing to hunt for: continue a chapter, clear revision, or fix a weak area.

For parents: Sit with your child for a minute: XP, day streak and revision due show how regularly they are studying.
Tip: Start every study session here: clear what is due for revision first, then continue your chapter.

Upgrade or Change PlanNEW

Menu: Upgrade Plan (top of the menu, above Dashboard) — it reads “Change Plan” on Max
Basic Plus Max

What you’ll find

  • Your current plan, monthly fee and number of subjects
  • The plans you can move to, each with its monthly fee: a higher plan shows what you get, a lower plan shows what you will no longer have
  • The exact amount to pay now and the date the new monthly fee starts — before you send anything
  • A short request form: why you want the change (required) and a few words in your own words
  • The status of your request: waiting, approved or not approved

How it works

  1. Tap Upgrade Plan at the top of the menu — it is there on every page.
  2. Choose the plan you want and read the amount shown.
  3. For an upgrade, send the amount on EasyPaisa or JazzCash (message us on WhatsApp for the payment details) and tick that you have sent it. Moving to a lower plan needs no payment now.
  4. Choose your reason, write a few words and send the request.
  5. W.H. Academy checks and approves it, and your plan changes on your dashboard.

How it helps

A family can move up when a student needs more, or down when less is needed, without waiting for a phone call. The reason you give helps the academy improve.

For parents: When you upgrade, you pay only the difference between the two plans’ monthly fees for the days left in that month (today counts). From the 1st of the next month, the full monthly fee of the new plan applies. Moving to a lower plan costs nothing now — the lower fee starts on the 1st of the next month.
Tip: Not sure which plan fits? Compare them in “Which plan includes what” at the top of this page.

Chapters & Games

Menu: Chapters
Basic Plus Max

What you’ll find

  • Your chapters, grouped by subject, with a search box
  • For each topic, 8 game types: Multiple Choice, Speed Quiz, Rapid Fire, True or False, Fill in the Blank, Match the Pairs, Sort into Groups, Put in Order
  • A Coming soon tag on chapters not ready yet

How it works

  1. Open Chapters and pick a chapter.
  2. Play the games for each topic. Every correct answer earns XP.
  3. Your accuracy per chapter builds up on the dashboard and in My Progress.
  4. Only the subjects you enrolled in are unlocked.

How it helps

Practice feels like play, so students practise more. Different game styles train recall, speed, matching and sequencing — not just guessing MCQs.

For parents: Your child can only open the subjects they are enrolled in, so the screen stays focused on the right syllabus.
Tip: Try a topic in different game styles — a recall game, a speed game and a matching game train different skills.

Boss Battle

Menu: Boss Battle
Basic Plus Max

What you’ll find

  • Three levels: Level 1 – Chapter, Level 2 – Subject, Level 3 – Class
  • The active paper for each level, plus a Past Papers shelf of earlier Boss Battle papers (practice only — not graded or ranked; part of Plus and Max)
  • Your submission status and, once released, your result
  • Badges for completing levels

How it works

  1. Open Boss Battle and choose Enter Battle on an available paper.
  2. Attempt it like a real test and submit.
  3. Your teacher reviews it and releases your result — you get an alert when it is ready.
  4. Keep your practice average high: it shows your readiness on the Ranks page.

How it helps

Students rehearse the real exam in a safe place, learn from teacher feedback, and build exam confidence level by level. For Middle (Class 6–8), the Boss Battle papers follow the APSACS paper pattern. If you prefer the Federal (FBISE) pattern instead, just request it and we will set it up on your dashboard.

For parents: Results are marked by a teacher, not by a machine. If a mark looks wrong you can ask for a recheck (see Support & Rechecking). Middle Boss Battle papers follow the APSACS pattern by default; the Federal pattern is available on request.
Tip: Finish the chapter games for a topic first. A strong practice average makes a Boss Battle feel like a rehearsal, not a surprise.

Revision

Menu: Revision
Basic Plus Max

What you’ll find

  • Due Now — questions ready to review today
  • Focus Areas — your weak topics
  • Wrong Answers Review — the questions you missed

How it works

  1. Open Revision. The number due also shows on your dashboard.
  2. Tap Start Revision Session and answer the questions.
  3. Questions come back at the right time so they stick in memory.
  4. A few minutes a day is enough.

How it helps

This is how knowledge turns into long-term memory. Students stop re-learning the same chapter before every exam.

For parents: A small daily revision habit beats a big cram session before the exam.
Tip: Do your due questions at the same time every day — a few minutes daily works better than one long weekly session.

My Progress

Menu: Progress
Basic Plus Max

What you’ll find

  • Accuracy by chapter and topic from the games you have played
  • Accuracy over time — weekly, monthly and lifetime
  • Compare several subjects on one chart; filter by class or subject

How it works

  1. Open Progress and choose a subject.
  2. Tap a chapter to see its topics.
  3. Read the lines on the chart: rising means improving.
  4. Use it to plan what to study next.

How it helps

Progress becomes visible, so students know exactly which chapter or topic needs work instead of guessing.

For parents: The clearest page for parents: it shows accuracy by chapter and how your child is trending week by week.
Tip: Once a week, find your weakest chapter and play its games again before moving on.

Ranks

Menu: Ranks
Basic Plus Max

What you’ll find

  • Your XP in each game type, with progress bars
  • Your average toward Boss Battle readiness (target: 70%)

How it works

  1. Open Ranks to see your totals.
  2. Play more games to fill the bars and raise your XP.
  3. Check the Boss Battle average before you take on a paper.

How it helps

A clear, friendly scoreboard that shows which game types are strong and which need more practice.

Tip: Use the readiness bar like a checklist: if it is below the target, play more games before the next Boss Battle.

Badges

Menu: Badges
Basic Plus Max

What you’ll find

  • Badges you have earned and those still locked
  • For every locked badge, what you need to do to unlock it

How it works

  1. Badges unlock automatically as you hit milestones: finishing chapters, keeping streaks, scoring well and more.
  2. Open Badges to see your collection.

How it helps

Small goals keep motivation high, especially for younger students.

Tip: Pick the nearest locked badge and make it this week’s goal.

AnimationsNEW

Menu: Animations (“Animate” on phones)
Basic Plus Max

What you’ll find

  • Animated, step-by-step solutions to the exercise questions from your book — the question on the left with a moving diagram, the full solution building up on the right
  • Theory concepts first, then the exercises, split into the same parts as the book (for Pythagoras: Theory · 7.1 · 7.2 · 7.3)
  • Sliders and drag-dots to change the numbers; the drawing changes live
  • Colour-coded parts, a 3D view you can rotate for real-world problems, themes, and thickness & text-size controls
  • Challenges at the end to test yourself

How it works

  1. Open Animations and pick a chapter, then a tab (Theory or an exercise) and a question.
  2. Watch the solution build step by step — Auto plays it like a video, Manual waits for you at every step.
  3. Change the numbers, recolour the parts, turn on the 3D view, or pick a calmer theme.
  4. Jump to any other question from the List, and try the challenges.

How it helps

Everywhere else you get static notes — words and a fixed picture. Here we animate your own book: students actually see how each exercise is solved and why a rule works, instead of memorising it. The library currently covers APSACS Mathematics and grows chapter by chapter; schools on other boards can have it customised to their own syllabus so the animations match the exact book the class uses. Only the subjects you enrolled in appear.

For parents: Animations are in English, designed for phones, and keep the student inside the lesson.
Tip: Before a test, replay the animation for any rule you have only memorised.

Notifications

Bell icon at the top-right of the Dashboard
Basic Plus Max

What you’ll find

  • Study reminders
  • Day-streak reminders
  • An alert when your Boss Battle result is ready

How it works

  1. Tap the bell and choose Enable device alerts, then tap Allow.
  2. Do this once on each device you use.
  3. iPhone/iPad (iOS 16.4+): add the site to the Home Screen first and open it from that icon.
  4. The in-app Guide lists the steps for Android, Windows and Mac.

How it helps

Students keep their streak alive and never miss a result — even when the app is closed.

Tip: Enable alerts on the device your child uses most.

Support & Rechecking

Menu: Support & Rechecking
Basic Plus Max

What you’ll find

  • My Rechecking Claims — open and resolved requests stay visible as history (Plus and Max)
  • A form to request a recheck of a graded Boss Battle paper (Plus and Max)
  • A Report an issue form that every plan can use

How it works

  1. Open Support & Rechecking.
  2. Find your paper using the Paper ID shown with your graded result.
  3. Submit your request with a short reason.
  4. Your teacher reviews it and updates the result if needed; you see the outcome here. The original marking is kept on record for a fair review.

How it helps

Students have a clear, respectful way to question a mark, and parents can see what happened.

Tip: Keep the Paper ID from the graded result handy — it makes the request quick.

Profile & Settings

Menu: Profile
Basic Plus Max

What you’ll find

  • Your details and subjects
  • Analytics: this week, this month and lifetime — accuracy, sessions, longest streak, certificates
  • Settings: themes (Light, Dark, Midnight, Ocean, Aurora, Forest, Gold, Sunset, Rose, Candy, Grape), text size and Change PIN

How it works

  1. Open Profile to see your numbers.
  2. Open Settings to pick a theme or a larger text size.
  3. To change your PIN, request a code on your registered email, enter it, and set the new PIN.
  4. To add or change subjects, contact W.H. Academy.

How it helps

Students make the dashboard comfortable to look at, and keep their account secure with their own PIN.

For parents: Your child’s PIN is private to them. Codes go only to the email address on file.
Tip: Check Settings once: a theme and text size that feel comfortable make studying easier.

Moving to the Next Class

Dashboard (shown when your class is promoted)
Basic Plus Max

What you’ll find

  • A short “You have been promoted” form on your dashboard
  • Your same Student ID and PIN continue

How it works

  1. When W.H. Academy promotes you, log in as usual.
  2. Choose your new class’s stream, subjects and plan and submit.
  3. Once W.H. Academy approves it (after fee confirmation), your new class opens.
  4. Your progress summary for the finished class is kept on record.

How it helps

No new account, no new ID, no lost history — the student simply continues.

For parents: You do not need to enrol again every year. See the Pricing page for current fees.
