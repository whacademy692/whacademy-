Fee questions, answered

How much does W.H. Academy cost?

The Dashboard fee depends on the class and the plan. Class 6–8 (Mathematics, Science, History, Geography and Computer) — all subjects per month: Basic ₨2,500, Plus ₨4,700, Max ₨5,500; or per subject: Basic ₨999, Plus ₨1,700, Max ₨2,500. Class 9–10 (all science subjects including Computer), per subject per month: Basic ₨1,200, Plus ₨1,999, Max ₨2,500. Class 11–12 (Mathematics and Physics), per subject per month: Basic ₨2,000, Plus ₨3,300, Max ₨3,999. Regular live online classes are optional and planned around your child's needs.

What is the Dashboard fee?

The Dashboard fee is the monthly fee shown on each plan card. It covers the W.H. Academy student dashboard and all the learning services listed in your plan. Regular live online classes are planned around your child's needs and their fee is confirmed with you on WhatsApp.

What is the difference between Basic, Plus and Max?

Every plan includes the full student dashboard and all our current games (chapter games, Boss Battle papers, smart revision, progress, ranks, XP, streaks, badges, certificates and study reminders). Plus adds Past Papers in Boss Battle, rechecking requests, recorded lectures, WhatsApp support, monthly mock tests and every advanced game as it launches. Max adds animations, weekly tests, past-paper sessions, detailed digital notes and a free live class every week in each of your subjects, on top of everything in Plus.

Which plan should I choose?

Max gives the complete experience: the full dashboard, every service, animations, weekly tests, past-paper sessions, detailed notes and a free live class every week in each of your subjects &mdash; the most effective way to prepare. Plus is a strong middle choice, and Basic is the simplest way to start.

How does pricing work for Class 6–8?

In Class 6–8 you can take all 5 subjects (Mathematics, Science, History, Geography and Computer) for one monthly fee: Basic ₨2,500, Plus ₨4,700, Max ₨5,500. If you only want some subjects, you can pay per subject instead: Basic ₨999, Plus ₨1,700, Max ₨2,500 per subject.

Which subjects are offered?

Class 6–8: Mathematics, Science, History, Geography and Computer. Class 9–10: all science subjects, including Computer. Class 11–12: Mathematics and Physics. In Class 6–8 you can take all subjects for one fee or pick your own per subject; in Class 9–12 the fee is per subject.

Is a live class really free with Max?

Yes. Max includes one free live online class every week in each subject you take, within the Dashboard fee. If you would like more regular live classes, the fee is planned around your child's needs and confirmed with you on WhatsApp.

Do I have to take regular live classes?

No. Regular live online classes are optional. You can learn with your plan alone and add live classes whenever you want.

How much do regular live classes cost?

The live-class fee is flexible and depends on your child’s needs: the class, the subjects and the number of classes per week. Message W.H. Academy on WhatsApp (03295215703); we confirm the fee for your needs and it joins your monthly Dashboard fee.

Is the Dashboard fee the same for everyone?

Yes. The Dashboard fee for each plan is the same for every student in the class.

Can I change my plan or add live classes later?

Yes. A student can move to a higher or a lower plan from the Upgrade Plan button on the Dashboard: choose the plan, see the exact amount, give a short reason and send the request — W.H. Academy approves it. See how plan changes work. Live classes are arranged on WhatsApp, and your new monthly total is confirmed with you before you pay.

What do I pay if I join or upgrade in the middle of a month?

Only for the days left in that month, counting today. For an upgrade you pay the difference between the two plans’ monthly fees for those days. From the 1st of the next month, the full monthly fee of your plan applies. Moving to a lower plan costs nothing now — the lower fee starts on the 1st of the next month.

Are these plans for international students?

No. International students have a different fee structure. It is decided according to each student’s needs, such as the syllabus they follow and the features they need. The features are the same as in these plans; only the fee structure differs. Message us on WhatsApp to get your fee.

Which board and classes do you cover?

Class 6 to 12. Plans are for Federal Board (FBISE) subjects. Students of other boards can request custom material, and the fee may differ.

What happens when my child moves to the next class?

No re-enrolment is needed. The Student ID and PIN stay the same. When W.H. Academy promotes the student, the dashboard asks them to choose the new class’s subjects and plan, and the new class opens once it is approved after fee confirmation.

Is home tuition included?

No. These fees are for online learning. Home tuition in Rawalpindi has its own fee, agreed with you — call or WhatsApp 03295215703.

How do I pay?

Pay via EasyPaisa or JazzCash to 0340‑0876011 (both on the same number), then send the payment screenshot on WhatsApp to confirm and activate your plan.
