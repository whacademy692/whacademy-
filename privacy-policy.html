Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

W.H. Academy ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our website at whacademypk.com/.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use W.H. Academy, the following information may be collected:

  • Enrollment: student name, email address, WhatsApp number, class, subjects, education board and plan selected.
  • Student account: Student ID, a securely stored (hashed) PIN, and learning progress such as quiz results, XP, streaks and certificates.
  • Feedback and demo requests: name, city, school or board, class and subject, your comments, and contact details if you provide them in the form.
  • Payment confirmation: fee payments are made by EasyPaisa or JazzCash, and the payment screenshot is shared with us on WhatsApp. We do not collect card details on this website.
  • Website usage: basic visit statistics on our homepage (see section 3).

Enrollment and student account data is stored in our database on Supabase. Feedback and demo-class requests are collected through Google Forms and stored in Google Sheets, which are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your student account and confirm your enrollment
  • To send account emails (confirmation, Student ID, PIN set-up codes)
  • To contact you regarding enrollment, fees and tutoring sessions
  • To show your learning progress to you and your teachers
  • To display student feedback/testimonials on our website (with your permission)
  • To improve our services based on feedback and usage

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Cookies & Analytics

Our homepage (whacademypk.com) uses Google Analytics to understand how many people visit the site and which pages they view. Google Analytics may set cookies and collect usage information such as pages visited, approximate location and device type. We do not use Google Analytics on the student dashboard, and we do not send student names, emails or quiz answers to it. You can block or opt out using your browser settings or the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. The student dashboard stores a login session in your browser so you stay signed in. External services (Google Fonts, Font Awesome CDN) may set their own cookies per their privacy policies.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase: database and backend for enrollment and student accounts
  • Resend: sending account and confirmation emails
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: bot protection on login and sign-up forms
  • Google Forms / Sheets: feedback and demo-class requests
  • Google Analytics: homepage visit statistics
  • Google Fonts: typography
  • Font Awesome (cdnjs): icons
  • GitHub Pages: website hosting
  • WhatsApp: support and payment confirmation

5. Data Security

Our website is served over HTTPS. Student PINs are stored in hashed form, and access to student records is limited to the academy team. All external links include security attributes. User-submitted content displayed on our site is sanitized before display. No online system is completely secure, so please never share your PIN or email verification codes with anyone, including over WhatsApp.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are designed for students of all ages including minors (Classes 6–12). We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information from children without parental consent. Enrollment forms are intended to be completed with parental awareness, and parents or guardians can contact us at any time to review or delete their child's data.

7. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your submitted feedback or enrollment data by contacting us at whacademy692@gmail.com. We will process such requests within 7 business days.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.
