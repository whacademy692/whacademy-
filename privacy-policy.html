Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

W.H. Academy ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and the privacy of your child. This policy explains in plain words what information we collect, why we collect it, who can see it, and what you can ask us to do with it. It applies to our website at whacademypk.com and the student dashboard and learning games.

1. Information We Collect

  • Enrollment details: the student's name, father's name, class, education board, subjects and plan chosen, WhatsApp / contact number, and email address if you give one.
  • Payment details: which fee was paid, how and when, and the payment screenshot you send us. We never ask for or store card or bank passwords on this website.
  • Learning activity: after enrollment, the student's progress and performance, such as quiz and game results, XP, ranks, streaks, badges, certificates, test marks and revision activity.
  • Account details: Student ID and a securely stored (hashed) PIN. We cannot read a PIN.
  • Feedback and demo requests: name, class, subject, your comments and contact details if you provide them.
  • Requests you make to us: for example a request to leave, to correct information, or to delete data, with the reason you give.
  • Basic technical information: basic website analytics and security logs (for example pages visited, device type and sign-in attempts), used to keep the service working and safe.

2. Why We Use Your Information

  • To contact you or your parent/guardian about enrollment, fees, classes and the student's account.
  • To keep the student's records, including enrollment, fee and attendance records.
  • To track the student's progress and to improve performance, for example by recommending what to practise next.
  • To send account messages such as confirmation, Student ID and PIN set-up codes.
  • To keep the service secure and to prevent misuse and spam.
  • To display feedback or a testimonial on our website, only with your permission.
  • To improve the website, notes and games.

3. Who Can See Your Information

  • The student sees their own account and progress.
  • The W.H. Academy team and the student's teachers can see the information needed to teach, support and manage the account.
  • Other students never see a student's real name or contact details. In game rankings only the student's game nickname is shown.
  • We use trusted service providers for hosting, messaging, email, forms and website security. They handle information only to provide those services for us.

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for advertising or marketing.

4. Cookies & Website Analytics

We use basic website analytics to understand how many people visit our public pages and which pages are useful. We do not send student names, contact details or quiz answers to analytics. The student dashboard keeps a sign-in session in your browser so that you stay signed in. Third-party fonts and icons used on our pages may set their own cookies. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

5. Data Security

Our website is served over HTTPS, PINs are stored in hashed form, sign-in is protected against automated attacks, and access to student records is limited to the academy team. No online system is completely secure. Please never share your PIN or verification codes with anyone, including over WhatsApp.

6. Children and Parental Consent

Our students are mostly under 18. For a student under 18, the enrollment form must be filled in by the parent or legal guardian, who confirms consent to the collection and use of the student's information as described here. A student aged 18 or over may consent for themselves. A parent or guardian can contact us at any time to see, correct or delete their child's information.

7. Leaving W.H. Academy

A student or parent may leave at any time. Log in to the student dashboard and go to Settings → Requests → Leave W.H. Academy, tell us briefly why, and send the request. You can also tell us on WhatsApp. Leaving does not return fees already paid (see the Fee & Refund Policy). After a student leaves, their records, including dashboard data, performance and game scores, may be kept for record-keeping and backup. They are not automatically erased.

8. Your Rights: Correction and Deletion

  • Correct information: if something is wrong (name, class, contact number and so on), use Settings → Requests → Correct my information in the dashboard, or message us on WhatsApp.
  • Delete data: you can ask us to delete the student's personal data from Settings → Requests → Delete my data, or by WhatsApp or email. After we confirm the request, personal data is deleted. We keep only the minimum fee and payment records that we need for accounting.
  • We reply to these requests within 7 business days.

9. How Long We Keep Information

We keep information while the student is enrolled and afterwards for record-keeping and backup, unless you ask us to delete personal data under section 8. Basic fee and payment records are kept for accounting.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it last changed. If we make an important change, we will tell students and parents on the dashboard or on WhatsApp.
